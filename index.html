Research

One theory line, two application domains.

Artificial brain models you can inspect

We build spiking and reservoir networks from individual neural recordings, then open them up — tracing which internal units carry which part of the answer, so the computation is legible instead of hidden.

Spiking neural networks Reservoir computing Explainable AI
raster · 40 units · —

Interfaces between brains and machines

Decoders that read intention and emotion from EEG and ECoG, applied to cursor and robot control, alternate speech for aphasia, and clinical systems for diagnosis, electroceuticals and digital therapeutics.

BCI / BMI Medical AI Electroceuticals Digital therapeutics
cursor control · hits 0

Forecasting systems that don't behave

Reservoir computing applied to chaotic and financial time series, with explainable features that show why a forecast moved — built for decisions where being wrong is expensive.

Time-series forecasting Financial data High-stakes decisions
